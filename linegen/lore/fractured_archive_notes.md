### Obligator
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Obligator
- Two-handed crush weapon, 85 Attack, rare drop. "Thrust" style hits several targets or bigger ones harder (5 ticks); "smash" is 3 ticks, single target.
- Charged with 1 ascension shard + 20 nature + 20 death runes per 10 charges; tradeable only when uncharged.
- Looks *(image)*: a dark grey-green spiked ball made of plates, green glowing between them, on a long brown vine. The uncharged one has less green showing.
- Lore (Rewards Poll Blog, developer): "While most Guthixians prefer to avoid conflict, the Guthixian Archivists obligation to defend the archive means they might have been known to wield a particularly powerful weapon from time to time." It began as the "TzHaar-Ket Breaker"; the Obligator look won the poll.
- Out Today calls it a "megarare".

### Ascension crossbows
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ascension_crossbows
- 80 Ranged, 2h, fires every 2 ticks on rapid, 6-tile range, only takes ascension bolts (plain, diamond, onyx). They always alternate which crossbow fires (Mod Hend, cited on the page).
- Looks *(image)*: two crystal crossbows in dark sea-green and bright teal.
- Lore: none in OSRS. The Wiki's trivia says Ocellus makes them in *RuneScape 3*; that isn't canon here.

### Zorya's tome
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Zorya%27s_tome
- 85 Magic, 2h book that works as a powered staff, 3-tick, base max hit 18; special attack "Zorya's flow" (50% energy). Charged with 3 astral + 2 nature runes per cast.
- Looks *(image)*: a large open book, tan or brown pages and cover, yellow (gold) corner caps, a green ribbon or bookmark.
- Lore (Rewards Poll Blog, developer): "an enchanted book full of magical wisdom that would make even the Wizard's Tower's inhabitants blush"; "a ... more Guthixian spin on the Gatekeeper's Cane". Who Zorya is: the Wiki's only note is from the *RuneScape* novel *The Gift of Guthix* (a Guardian of Guthix who guarded the rune essence mine). Not in-game in OSRS, so I left it out of the facts.

### Rondache
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Rondache
- Shield, 80 Defence, +8 Strength (equal to the Avernic defender), +2 flat armour and magic resistance. Mitigated damage charges it, up to 20; special "Shield Slam" uses all charges.
- Looks *(image)*: round, pale silver rim, gold and blue bands, dark centre with a gold four-pointed star, gold studs.
- Lore (Rewards Poll Blog, quoted on the page): "The Rondache is an ancient Icyene artefact, wielded in Saradomin's name to use enemy blows against them by absorbing damage..."

### Living record (tiers 1 to 5)
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Living_record_(tier_1) (and `_(tier_2)` to `_(tier_5)`)
- Cosmetic cape, options "Wear, Change-style, Drop". Tier 1 at 100 completions, claimed from the Records room at the south end of the archive behind the Strange door, opened with the Archive key (dropped by the Archive Guardian on the first kill after 100 raids). Tiers 2 to 5 (500, 1,000, 1,500, 2,000) "from an NPC at the Fractured Archive"; the Wiki doesn't name that NPC.
- Engravings: tier 1 "Scrivener", 2 "Salvager", 3 "Prospector", 4 "Reliquist", 5 "Antisqüien".
- Looks *(image)*: tier 1 is pale green with a darker olive collar and a faint face; tier 5 is mossy olive with a skull face at the top and ragged tails edged in purple. Tiers 2 to 4 not checked.

### Archivist's folio
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Archivist%27s_folio
- Untradeable, not worn. Charged with ascension shards (5 teleports a shard); teleports to the lobby.
- Looks *(image)*: a green-bound book with a carved skull and branching roots, the skull's eyes bright green.
- Lore: the examine; and (First Rewards Proposal) "Archivists used these to get back in a pinch, and since there don't seem to be any Archivists around any more...". Everything You Need To Know: "left behind by archivists from long ago."

### Horn of Bran
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Horn_of_Bran and https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Transcript:Horn_of_Bran

- Looks *(image)*: a long curved horn, brown, with a pale rim and tip.
- Inspect: "You notice a small symbol, similar to the one you've seen the Knights of the Round Table wear."
- Cutscene quotes: Arthur: "Bran's bloody horn... By God, now that's not something I expected to see... Ever!"; the knights' overheads "Bran?!", "The horn of Bran?", "Did someone say Bran?!"; Lancelot "Settle down everybody."; Tristram "Yes! And finally, some good booze! They just don't make ale here like they do back home."; Arthur "You have brought us something of ours, and you shall receive something of ours in return. A blessing from our home." Player (raid done): "I found the horn within the inner vault, Your Majesty. After defeating Guthix's corrupt guardian." The Wiki's conditions for the two player stories are marked unknown.

### Elemental fragments
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Elemental_fragments
- Untradeable, "Break" to raise the base max hit of that element's spells by 2 for good. Has duplicate protection.
- Looks *(image)*, fire only: a red flame inside a ring of thorny vine with orange four-pointed stars. The other three not checked.
- Lore (developer, First Rewards Proposal and Rewards Poll Blog): "Consume a tear that Guthix shed in remembrance of an element..."

Balance... through... death..."; player: "...have some ascension juice and shut up."; "Mmm... Ascension juice." Overhead when picked up: "Ascension!" Menagerie fights: "*slap*", "Grrr!".

## 3. The story

- **Who built it, and why.** Main page: "The Archive was built by followers of Guthix in order to store Guthix's knowledge and items at the bottom of the Chasm of Tears. At some point, work on the Archive was to be halted indefinitely for a higher priority." The Grimy note (https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Transcript:Grimy_note) reads: "Work on the Archive is to be abandoned. We must hope that the progress already made will be enough. There is a higher priority now and it will require every resource we have. Guthix guide us." Jagex's First Look says instead "created by Guthix himself in order to house his most treasured and intriguing possessions", "assembled by Guthix himself over the course of millennia". The First Rewards Proposal adds that it is "a collection of Guthix's most-prized artefacts from a variety of factions and cultures across Gielinor." Ivy: "This Archive was built to house various other powerful artefacts, to stop them falling into the wrong hands."
- **The guardian.** "A guardian was left within the archive to defend it." First Look: "The Archive Guardian has lain dormant for thousands of years and is less than pleased to have been awoken." Alliance log: "the company encountered the dormant guardian. It is a tragedy that it had to be destroyed but it could not recognise friend from foe."
- **How Lucien opened it.** Main page: "The Archive would remain undisturbed for thousands of years until the events of While Guthix Sleeps, where the Mahjarrat Lucien was able to claim the Stone of Jas from the nearby Ancient Guthixian Temple, consequentially opening the entrance to the Archive." Miniquest description: "The Mahjarrat Lucien's meddling with the Stone of Jas has opened an ancient entrance to a cache of Guthix's most prized possessions". The Blocked cavity outside the temple becomes the "Opening" after the quest; its internal name is `luc2_raid_entry`.
- **The alliance.** "the alliance of the Temple Knights, Guardians of Armadyl, and Crux Eqal". Leaders on the ground: Eva Cashien (Temple Knights), Armacus Selles (Guardians of Armadyl), Ivy Sophista (Crux Eqal; Crux Eqal's founder is Thaerisk). General Montoya runs the lobby. News: "the Alliance that you helped assemble during While Guthix Sleeps".
- **The gods' agents.** Lucien's forces under Moia (Zamorakian side via Lucien); the Dagon'hai, split from Lucien; Zamorakian mages (not Dagon'hai) who summoned Dreadnoth; Bork's Bandosian goblin warband; Ezekiel for Saradomin; Xarth, a tormented demon; and finally Ocellus, a Guardian of Guthix.
- **The miniquest.** Ivy, Eva and Armacus meet the player at the entrance; their troops are still on the way. Six vulnerable chambers; the central chamber is held by a Guthixian creation. The player clears the Nexus, then one of each pair of side bosses, while the alliance takes the others. After three sets of intruders, the Navigator reports a disturbance in the sealed inner vault. A Hooded Figure there is Ocellus: "He charged me... to guard the source of his power... Your alliance... is false... Armadyl... Saradomin... They are no better... than the rest..."; "Only Guthix himself is worthy... but he is long gone... To keep his power safe... I will take his place... The time has come... for ascension." After the fight: Ivy names Guthix's guardians, "There is Juna of course, but also Valluta... Aeternam... ... the stories call him the Guardian of Ascension." The player describes "a sword of some sorts. It had been shattered, but Ocellus was still able to use it." Ivy: "Ocellus will have been granted power by Guthix himself. He will not die easily." Eva: "I fear our defence of the Archive has only just begun." Per the Ocellus page, the void severed his left arm when he tried the Blade one last time.
- **The Blade.** https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Blade: "an ancient and powerful artefact that was once wielded by Guthix", entrusted broken to Ocellus; "lightly sentient ... seemingly capable of moving on its own." Hanging examine: "It's broken and hanging from a vine, but power still seems to radiate from it."
